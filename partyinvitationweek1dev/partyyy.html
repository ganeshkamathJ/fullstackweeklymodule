Welcome Heroes & Citizens of Gotham!

Bruce Wayne and Batman invite every citizen, superhero, ally, and defender of justice to the most exclusive celebration and charity gala ever held at Wayne Manor and the Batcave.

Enjoy gourmet dining, Batmobile tech displays, crime-fighting gadgets, live orchestra, cosplay contest, and unlimited superhero fun!

About The Event

What is this Event?

The Bat-Family has successfully protected Gotham City from major crime sprees once again! To celebrate peace in Gotham and raise funds for the Thomas & Martha Wayne Foundation, Batman (Bruce Wayne) has organized this grand annual gala.

Everyone is welcome! Whether you are a vigilante, a superhero, a detective, a tech enthusiast, or simply a fan of DC Comics, there is something exciting for everyone.
